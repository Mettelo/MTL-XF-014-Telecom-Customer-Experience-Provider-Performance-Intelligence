# Project Brief

## Client Commission
Customer attrition and service dissatisfaction are being discussed across broadband, mobile and pay-TV teams, but leadership lacks one consistent view that separates provider scale from complaint performance and persistent deterioration.

The team has been commissioned to answer:

> **Which services and provider-performance patterns warrant intervention, and where should customer-experience teams prioritise root-cause investigation?**

## Primary Dataset
**Ofcom Telecoms and Pay-TV Complaints — quarterly underlying data**  
Publisher: Ofcom  
Official source: https://www.ofcom.org.uk/telecoms-and-pay-tv-complaints

## Required Delivery Lifecycle
**Discovery → Requirements → Governance → Acquisition → Engineering → Data Quality → Analysis → Decision Framework → BI/Product → Assurance/UAT → Executive Recommendation → Handover**

## Cross-Functional Roles
| Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Map customer-experience decisions, requirements and intervention scenarios. |
| Data Governance Analyst | Define provider/service reference data, KPI definitions and quality controls. |
| Data Engineer | Build recurring quarterly ingestion and standardised history. |
| Customer Insight/Data Analyst | Benchmark providers, trends and persistent deterioration. |
| BI Developer | Build executive provider-performance and service dashboards. |
| Product Analyst | Translate findings into product/service investigation hypotheses. |

## Core Business Questions
1. What is the current baseline and where is material variation concentrated?
2. Which definitions and data-quality issues could change the decision?
3. What segments, organisations, products, areas or cohorts require deeper investigation?
4. Which indicators are appropriate for prioritisation and which are not?
5. What intervention options are supported by evidence?
6. How should management monitor the decision after handover?

## Success Criteria
- traceable requirements and acceptance criteria;
- governed definitions, ownership, lineage and quality rules;
- reproducible ingestion/transformation;
- defensible complaint rates, provider benchmarking, service-type differences, persistent deterioration, trend breaks and customer-experience priorities;
- transparent prioritisation logic;
- useful decision-support product;
- UAT/assurance evidence;
- executive recommendation and handover;
- visible team contribution.

## Review Panel
Mettelo reviewers may challenge requirements, definitions, quality, analytical assumptions, thresholds, model use, governance, usability, recommendations and maintainability.
