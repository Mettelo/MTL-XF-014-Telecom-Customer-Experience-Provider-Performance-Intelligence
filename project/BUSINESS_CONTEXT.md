# Business Context

## Simulated Client
**Confidential UK Telecom Strategy & Customer Experience Directorate**

## Business Pressure
Customer attrition and service dissatisfaction are being discussed across broadband, mobile and pay-TV teams, but leadership lacks one consistent view that separates provider scale from complaint performance and persistent deterioration.

## Executive Decision
**Which services and provider-performance patterns warrant intervention, and where should customer-experience teams prioritise root-cause investigation?**

## Stakeholders
- **Customer Experience Director:** accountable sponsor and decision owner.
- **Operational/Service Lead:** validates practical usability.
- **Data Owner/Steward:** challenges source authority, quality and definitions.
- **Finance/Risk/Performance stakeholder:** tests materiality and intervention logic.
- **Data & Analytics Lead:** owns technical quality and maintainability.
- **End users:** participate in UAT.

## Delivery Roles
| Role | Expected contribution |
|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Map customer-experience decisions, requirements and intervention scenarios. |
| Data Governance Analyst | Define provider/service reference data, KPI definitions and quality controls. |
| Data Engineer | Build recurring quarterly ingestion and standardised history. |
| Customer Insight/Data Analyst | Benchmark providers, trends and persistent deterioration. |
| BI Developer | Build executive provider-performance and service dashboards. |
| Product Analyst | Translate findings into product/service investigation hypotheses. |

The intended organisational outcome is a trusted, repeatable decision process rather than a one-off report.
