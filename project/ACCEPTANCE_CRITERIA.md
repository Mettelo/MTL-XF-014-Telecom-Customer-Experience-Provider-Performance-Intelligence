# Agreed Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC1:** Complaint rates are stored and displayed with the exact published unit/denominator.
2. **AC2:** At least four quarters are used where available to distinguish persistence from a one-quarter spike.
3. **AC3:** Provider/service changes affecting comparability are recorded in a continuity log.
4. **AC4:** At least five dashboard values reconcile to Ofcom source data for selected provider/period combinations.
5. **AC5:** No overall provider score combines unrelated services without an explicit approved weighting method.
6. **AC6:** Priority flags require documented persistence/materiality rules.
7. **AC7:** The dashboard clearly states that Ofcom complaint rates are not a complete measure of customer satisfaction.
8. **AC8:** A reviewer can rebuild the quarterly provider/service history and reproduce the priority segmentation from the documented source files.

## Sign-off Rule
Files existing is not sufficient. The review panel must verify these criteria from repository evidence.
